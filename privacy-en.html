Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026 · Version 2.0

1. Who we are

Kai is an AI assistant that configures and manages Kommo CRM accounts on the user's instructions, delivered through the Telegram bot @aiicrm_bot (the "Service"). The data controller for the processing described in this Policy is Tsikhan Varabyou, the operator of the Service ("we", "us").

You can reach us about anything in this Policy at ai.crm.2000@gmail.com or via Telegram at @aiicrm_bot. We answer data-protection requests from the same channels we operate the Service on, so no separate portal or form is required.

2. What this Policy covers

This Policy applies to personal data processed when you interact with the bot, connect your Kommo account to it, or pay for a subscription. It does not govern Kommo itself, Telegram itself, or any other service you use alongside Kai — each of those has its own privacy terms.

3. Data we process, and where it comes from

We deliberately keep the data footprint narrow: the Service only handles what is needed to act on your instructions inside your CRM. Specifically:

CategoryDetailsSource
Kommo connection data OAuth 2.0 access and refresh tokens (stored encrypted) and your Kommo account address (subdomain) Kommo, when you authorize the integration
Telegram identity Your Telegram user ID and username Telegram, when you start the bot
Content you send the bot Text messages, voice messages, screenshots and other images, and Excel/CSV files you upload for import You
CRM working data Objects the Service reads or creates in your Kommo account on your request (pipelines, stages, fields, contacts, tasks, automations) and the plans generated from your answers You; your Kommo account
Billing data Subscription status and payment confirmations. We never receive or store full card numbers — card data is handled entirely by the payment provider Payment providers
We never ask for, receive, or store your Kommo login or password. Access to your CRM works exclusively through OAuth 2.0, and you can revoke it at any moment in your Kommo integration settings — the token is invalidated immediately.

4. Why we process it, and on what legal basis

Under Article 6 of the GDPR, each processing operation rests on one of the following bases:

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) — connecting your Kommo account, interpreting your messages, executing the CRM actions you request, importing the files you upload, and managing your subscription. This is the core of the Service: without this processing, Kai cannot do what you ask it to do.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) — keeping technical logs needed to diagnose failures, prevent abuse of the bot, and secure the Service. We log the minimum required for these purposes and do not use logs for profiling.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — any processing that is genuinely optional, such as product announcements sent through the bot. You can withdraw consent at any time without affecting the Service.

We do not sell personal data, do not share it with advertisers, and do not use it to train our own or anyone else's models.

5. Subprocessors and international transfers

Kai relies on a small set of processors, each receiving only what its function requires:

ProcessorWhat it receivesPurpose
Kommo API The API calls needed to execute your instructions in your account Reading and writing CRM objects you asked for
AI providers (OpenAI, Anthropic) The text of your requests and, for voice messages, the audio for transcription. Your OAuth tokens and credentials are never included in these calls Understanding your messages and generating responses and plans
Payment providers (card processing, Telegram Stars, USDT crypto payments) The data each provider needs to process your payment Subscription billing. Card details stay with the processor; we only see the confirmation

Some of these providers are located outside the European Economic Area. Where personal data leaves the EEA, the transfer is safeguarded by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an equivalent lawful transfer mechanism maintained by the provider.

6. How long we keep data

  • OAuth tokens — until you disconnect the integration or delete your data; revoked tokens are invalidated immediately and purged.
  • Conversation history and generated plans — while your account with the bot is active, so context is preserved between sessions; deleted on request.
  • Uploaded files (Excel/CSV, images) — only as long as needed to complete the operation you requested (for example an import), then removed.
  • Voice messages — sent for transcription and not retained after the text transcript is produced.
  • Billing records — for the period required by accounting and tax law applicable to us.
  • Technical logs — for a short rolling window sufficient for security and debugging, after which they are overwritten.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you may, at any time:

  1. Access the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy;
  2. Rectify inaccurate data;
  3. Erase your data — write to ai.crm.2000@gmail.com or use the /delete command in the bot;
  4. Receive your data in a portable, machine-readable format;
  5. Object to processing based on legitimate interest;
  6. Lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority — for EEA residents, the data protection authority of your member state.
When you request deletion, we remove your conversation history, OAuth tokens, generated plans, and the link between your Telegram account and your Kommo account. Objects already created inside your Kommo account (pipelines, fields, contacts and so on) belong to that account and remain there — you manage them through Kommo directly.

Revoking CRM access does not require contacting us at all: disable the integration in your Kommo settings and the Service loses access instantly.

8. Security

OAuth tokens are stored encrypted at rest. Transport between the bot, our servers, Kommo, and every subprocessor uses TLS. Access to production data is restricted to the operator and is not shared with third parties outside the subprocessors listed above. We follow a data-minimisation approach: the Service does not request Kommo permissions it does not use, and does not copy your CRM database to our servers — it operates on your data inside your account, on your instruction.

9. Children

The Service is a business tool and is not directed at anyone under 16. We do not knowingly process minors' data; if you believe a minor has used the Service, contact us and we will delete the associated data.

10. Changes to this Policy

When we materially change this Policy, we will notify active users through the bot and update the version and date at the top of this page. The current version is always available at this address. Continued use of the Service after a change takes effect constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.
