HubSpot AI assistant: set up your CRM from a chat message

Updated November 4, 2026 · Kai Team · 5 min read

An AI assistant for HubSpot is a chat where you describe your sales process in plain words and it works with HubSpot through the official API: it shows pipelines and deals, creates deals, contacts, tasks and notes and reviews the funnel. Kai does this in Telegram or in the browser. Today HubSpot connects through the API as a custom CRM; one-click connection is in development.

TL;DR

  • Today: a HubSpot service key plus Kai's ready-made spec, the custom CRM connection through the API, on the Max plan.
  • Works: pipelines and stages, deals (list, search, create, change the stage and amount), contacts, tasks and notes on a deal, funnel analytics.
  • In development: a Kai app for HubSpot with a "Sign in with HubSpot" button and an install link, with no keys or JSON.
  • Not yet: creating pipelines and fields, companies, automations on HubSpot events.

How to connect HubSpot to Kai today

  1. In HubSpot open Development → Keys → Service keys and click Create service key. A super admin or a user with the Developer tools permission can create it.
  2. Name the key "Kai" and add the scopes crm.objects.deals.read, crm.objects.deals.write, crm.schemas.deals.read, crm.objects.contacts.read, crm.objects.contacts.write. Click Create and copy the key.
  3. Open the custom CRM connection page, section "Ready-made specs: HubSpot", copy the JSON and paste the key into the token field.
  4. In the bot @aiicrm_bot or the web chat: /connect → "My own CRM via API" → paste the JSON. Kai checks the access and shows your deal pipelines.
A service key acts on behalf of the HubSpot account, so do not publish it. You can rotate or delete it in HubSpot, and the old key stops working.

What Kai does in HubSpot

  • Pipelines and stages. Shows deal pipelines and stages and counts deals per stage.
  • Deals. Finds a deal by name, creates a new one in the right pipeline and stage, changes the stage and the amount.
  • Contacts. Creates a contact with a phone and an email.
  • Tasks and notes. Creates a task with a due date and adds a note to a deal.
  • Analytics. How many deals sit at each stage of a pipeline.

Every action goes through the official HubSpot API. Everything created stays in your HubSpot account whether or not you keep using the assistant.

FAQ

Is there an AI assistant that works with HubSpot from a chat message?

Yes, Kai. Today it connects to HubSpot through the API as a custom CRM: it sees pipelines and deals, creates deals, contacts, tasks and notes, moves deals between stages and reviews the funnel. One-click connection is in development.

How do I connect HubSpot to Kai today?

Create a key in HubSpot: Development → Keys → Service keys, with access to deals and contacts. Take the ready-made HubSpot spec from the custom CRM page, paste the key into it and send it to Kai: /connect → "My own CRM via API". Connecting a custom CRM is part of the Max plan.

When will one-click HubSpot connection be available?

It is in development: a Kai app for HubSpot with a "Sign in with HubSpot" button and an install link, with no keys or JSON. As soon as it launches, Kai will tell you in the chat.

What does Kai not do in HubSpot yet?

It does not create pipelines or fields, does not work with companies and does not run automations on HubSpot events. All of this is planned together with the one-click connection.
