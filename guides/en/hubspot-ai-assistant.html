HubSpot AI assistant: set up your CRM from a chat message

Updated November 16, 2026 · Kai Team · 5 min read

An AI assistant for HubSpot is a chat where you describe your sales process in plain words and it builds it in HubSpot through the official API: pipelines and stages, properties, deals, contacts, companies, tasks and notes. Kai does this in Telegram or in the browser. HubSpot support is in beta: one-click connection, structure, data, analytics and event automations already work.

TL;DR

  • Install from the link app.aii-crm.com/install/hubspot: HubSpot asks for permission, then Kai links the HubSpot account to your account in Telegram or the web chat. No keys, no JSON.
  • Works: pipelines and stages, deal properties with option lists, deals, contacts, companies, tasks, notes, loss reasons and lead sources, funnel analytics, event automations.
  • Not yet: tags (HubSpot itself has none), inviting users and roles, buttons or panels inside HubSpot.
  • Available from the Start plan, €29 per month; automations start on the Pro plan. The first 3 days are a trial.

How to connect HubSpot to Kai

  1. Open the install link and sign in to HubSpot if it asks.
  2. Pick the HubSpot account and check which data Kai asks to access. Keep the permissions for companies and properties ticked: without them Kai can't manage companies and properties. Allow the access.
  3. On the "Kai is installed in HubSpot" page press "Open Kai in Telegram" or "Continue in the browser". The link works once, for 30 minutes: it ties this HubSpot account to your Kai account.
  4. Kai confirms the connection and asks what to set up.

One more way, right in the bot @aiicrm_bot or the web chat: the /connect command → "HubSpot (beta)" → "Sign in with HubSpot".

Signing in with HubSpot uses OAuth: Kai never sees your HubSpot password. You can disconnect Kai in HubSpot, in the list of connected apps, and the access is gone at once.

What Kai sets up in HubSpot

  • Pipelines and stages. A new pipeline with stages from one message; add, rename or delete a stage. How many pipelines you can have is set by your HubSpot plan.
  • Properties. Custom deal properties, including option lists, and their values on a deal.
  • Deals. Create in the right pipeline and stage, move between stages, change the amount, search; a link to the deal record in HubSpot.
  • Contacts and companies. Create them and link them to a deal.
  • Tasks and notes. A task with a due date on a deal and a note, straight from the chat.
  • Funnel analytics. Deals per stage, loss reasons, the "health" of a specific deal.
  • Event automations. A deal is created or moves to a stage, and Kai creates a task or sends a notification.

Every action goes through the official HubSpot API. Everything created stays in your HubSpot account whether or not you keep using the assistant.
