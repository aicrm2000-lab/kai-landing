Pipedrive AI assistant: set up your CRM from a chat message

Updated November 3, 2026 · Kai Team · 5 min read

An AI assistant for Pipedrive is a chat where you describe your sales process in plain words and it builds it in Pipedrive through the official API: pipelines and stages, fields, deals, people, organizations, activities and notes. Kai does this in Telegram or in the browser. Pipedrive support is in beta: structure, data, activities and analytics work; automations on Pipedrive events come next.

TL;DR

  • Install from the link app.aii-crm.com/install/pipedrive: Pipedrive asks for permission, then Kai links the company to your account in Telegram or the web chat.
  • Works: pipelines and stages, deal, person and organization fields with option lists, deals, people, organizations, activities, notes, users, funnel analytics with won and lost deals.
  • Not yet: rules on Pipedrive events (Kai's automations and integrations) and buttons or panels inside Pipedrive.
  • Available from the Start plan, €29 per month. The first 3 days are a trial.

How to connect Pipedrive to Kai

  1. Open the install link and sign in to Pipedrive if it asks.
  2. Pipedrive shows which data Kai asks to access. Press Allow.
  3. On the "Kai is installed in Pipedrive" page press "Open Kai in Telegram" or "Continue in the browser". The link works once, for 30 minutes: it ties this Pipedrive company to your Kai account.
  4. Kai confirms the connection and asks what to set up.

Two more ways, right in the bot @aiicrm_bot or the web chat: the /connect command → "Pipedrive (beta)" → "Sign in with Pipedrive", or send a personal API token: Pipedrive → avatar → Personal preferences → API tab.

Signing in with Pipedrive uses OAuth: Kai never sees your Pipedrive password. An API token acts on your behalf, so do not publish it. Regenerate it in the same settings and the old one stops working.

What Kai sets up in Pipedrive

  • Pipelines and stages. A new pipeline with stages from one message; add, rename or delete a stage.
  • Fields. Custom fields on deals, people and organizations, including option lists, and their values on a deal.
  • Deals. Create, move between stages, change the value and the owner, search; a link to the deal card in Pipedrive.
  • People and organizations. Create them and link them to a deal.
  • Activities and notes. An activity with a due date on a deal and a note, straight from the chat.
  • Funnel analytics. Deals per stage, won and lost deals, the "health" of a specific deal.

Every action goes through the official Pipedrive API. Everything created stays in your Pipedrive company whether or not you keep using the assistant.

FAQ

Is there an AI assistant that sets up Pipedrive from a chat message?

Yes, Kai. You describe your sales process in plain words in Telegram or the browser, and Kai creates pipelines and stages, fields, deals, people, organizations, activities and notes in Pipedrive through the official API and reviews the funnel. Pipedrive support is in beta.

How do I connect Pipedrive to Kai?

Open the install link app.aii-crm.com/install/pipedrive, allow access in Pipedrive and on the "Kai is installed" page press "Open Kai in Telegram" or "Continue in the browser". Two more ways in the bot: /connect → Pipedrive (beta) → "Sign in with Pipedrive", or a personal API token from your Pipedrive settings.

Can Kai build automations in Pipedrive?

Not yet. Rules on Pipedrive events (a deal moves to a stage → a task or a notification) and event-driven integrations come after the beta. Today Kai sets up the structure, manages data and activities and reviews the funnel.

Which plan do I need for Pipedrive?

Any plan from Start, €29 per month for one CRM. The first 3 days are a trial without a card, enough to test Kai on your own Pipedrive company.
